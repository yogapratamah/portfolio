01
Design Operations & Automation

Scaling B2B White-Label POS via Figma Variables

+

The internal operations workflow for onboarding new white-label POS clients depended on manually rebuilt UI kits for every merchant theme — slow, error-prone, and hard to maintain at scale.

Migrated the system to a multi-tier token architecture in Figma Variables: primitive tokens for raw values, semantic tokens for intent, and a component layer that resolved automatically per client theme.

The result cut client onboarding time from 38 hours down to 75 minutes, and removed the design bottleneck from the sales pipeline entirely.
